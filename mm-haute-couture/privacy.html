Privacy Policy

1. Commitment to Privacy

Your privacy is important to us. It is Márcia Morais Haute Couture's policy to respect your privacy regarding any information we may collect from you across our website and private fitting studio.

2. Information Collection

We only ask for personal information (such as name, email, phone/WhatsApp, and event date) when we truly need it to provide a bespoke consultation or tailoring service to you. We collect it by fair and lawful means, with your knowledge and consent.

3. Data Retention & Security

We only retain collected information for as long as necessary to provide you with your requested fitting service. What data we store, we protect within commercially acceptable means to prevent loss and theft, as well as unauthorized access or disclosure.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share any personally identifying information publicly or with third parties, except when required to do so by law in Queensland, Australia.

5. Contact Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions about how we handle user data and personal information, feel free to contact us via WhatsApp at +61 401 185 923.
