September 2026 | Brisbane

Queensland Brazil Week

In its 5th edition, Queensland Brazil Week celebrated Brazilian culture, creativity, and talent in Australia. Márcia Morais Haute Couture was present at this important gathering, bringing the sophistication of Brazilian haute couture to Brisbane.

The participation highlighted the brand's identity, recognized for its artisanal care, precision in finishing, and attention to detail that transforms every creation into a unique piece.

More than just showcasing Brazilian fashion, the experience represented an opportunity to value the national culture and strengthen ties between Brazil and Australia, reaffirming the essence of Márcia Morais Haute Couture: elegance, exclusivity, and excellence in every detail.
